Pray Shield: Put God first Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 28, 2026

Pray Shield: Put God first is designed to keep prayer content private. The app stores prayer sessions, reflections, goals, reminders, and selected app-lock preferences on your device unless you choose to use system or purchase services that require Apple or RevenueCat processing.

Information We Process

  • Prayer history, mood check-ins, relationship check-ins, reminders, goals, and favorites are stored locally on your device.
  • Screen Time and Family Controls permissions are used only to let you choose apps to block until you pray. Pray Shield: Put God first does not receive your full device activity history.
  • Subscription status and purchase events are processed through Apple and RevenueCat to unlock paid features and restore purchases.
  • Anonymous product analytics may be processed to understand reliability and feature usage. Prayer text, private reflections, prayer requests, selected app names, Screen Time tokens, contact details, and precise location are not sent to analytics.

How We Use Information

  • To provide Pray Shield, guided prayers, Bible references, streaks, reminders, and purchase access.
  • To restore subscriptions and prevent duplicate purchase issues.
  • To improve app reliability and product quality through anonymous product analytics.

Third-Party Services

Pray Shield: Put God first may use Apple StoreKit, Apple Screen Time APIs, RevenueCat for subscription management, and Mixpanel or similar analytics services for anonymous product analytics. These services process only the information needed for their function.

Contact

For privacy questions, open a support request at GitHub Support.
